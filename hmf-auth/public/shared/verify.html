What is checked

  • The doctor is active, registered as a doctor and has a license number
  • The patient exists and their identity matches
  • The prescription is active and not past its expiry date
  • Doses are within safe single and daily limits
  • No clash with the patient's recorded allergies
  • No duplicate or interacting medicines on other active prescriptions
  • Controlled medicines: no refills, 30-day validity, ID check
